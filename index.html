      About this site

      Kanji Steps is a free study tool for the JLPT N5 kanji set. The JLPT does not publish an official kanji list; the 103 kanji shown here are the commonly used N5 list compiled from the legacy pre-2010 test specification and used by major study sites and textbooks. Readings and meanings are taken from data cross-checked entry-by-entry against KANJIDIC.
